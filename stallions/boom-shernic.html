About Boom Shernic

Boom Shernic — 2004 Sorrel Stallion, AQHA, 14.3 hands. Standing at Silver Spurs Equine LLC, Scottsdale AZ. Full breeding information, video, and booking at silverspursequine.com.

He is the leading money earner sired by Boomernic, the NRHA Hall of Fame stallion standing alongside him on this roster. When Quarter Horse News reported Boomernic’s death in 2014, Boom Shernic was named first among his get. Boomernic is buried at the Arizona ranch, and Michael Miola commissioned a life-size bronze in his honor.

The defining night came at the 2010 National Reining Breeders Classic in Katy, Texas, thirteen years after Boomernic had sired his first NRBC champion. Drawn 22nd in a Level 4 Open Derby final of 31 pairs, Craig Schmersal knew the mark to beat was a 227. He ran a 228. Jordan Larson followed three horses later on Stop Like A Dream and scored 227.5; two-time NRBC champion Shawn Flarida came next on Wimpys Little Chic, the NRHA’s leading money earner, and also marked 227.5. The win added $75,000 against an NRBC purse that exceeded $1.4 million that year.

“He’s one of my all-time favorites.” — Craig Schmersal, after the 2010 NRBC Open Derby Championship

The same season he was NRHA Open Derby Reserve Champion on a 234, EquiStat’s #1 ranked 4/5/6-year-old reining horse, and a USEF Bronze Medalist named to the United States team for the Alltech FEI World Equestrian Games in Kentucky. He did not make the trip: he was not feeling completely right and the decision was made to withhold him rather than risk injury. By December 2011 he stood as the fifth highest earning horse in the NRHA.

Silver Spurs bills him as a non-pro’s stallion, and his get record supports it. Of his leading offspring, the great majority of the titles are in Non Pro, Limited Non Pro and Intermediate Non Pro divisions: Xtra Kiss Me Again at $140,777, Boom Shine Boom at $57,164, Theo Boom Shernic at $48,008 winning the NRHA Germany 4YO Breeders Futurity Non Pro, and Blazing N My Boomers taking the AQHA World Amateur Ranch Riding Championship. Total offspring earnings exceed $583,000.

His dam, She And Chic Dunit, is by Smart Chic Olena, which puts Smart Little Lena behind the bottom of his pedigree and Boomernic’s Reminic-based power on top. For a non-pro or amateur rider looking for a horse with a manageable mind and open-level ability, that combination at this fee is the argument.
