About Epic Titan

Epic Titan — 2013 Sorrel Stallion, registered AQHA and APHA, 14.3 hands. Known to his followers simply as “Titan.” Standing at Silver Spurs Equine LLC, Scottsdale AZ. Full breeding information, video, and booking at silverspursequine.com.

Titan is unusual among reining stallions in that a large audience watched him grow up. Clinton Anderson documented his training in the video series “Titan: A Legend in the Making,” released through the Downunder Horsemanship YouTube channel, which followed a reining prospect from the early stages through his show career. In 2018 Anderson announced a partnership with Michael Miola and Silver Spurs to campaign the then five-year-old stallion.

“About every 10 years, a horse comes along that changes an industry.” — Clinton Anderson, on partnering with Silver Spurs to campaign Epic Titan, 2018

The show record followed quickly. He won the 2018 Cactus Reining Classic on a 234, was Reserve Champion in the 2018 NRBC Open Derby on a 225.5, and Reserve Champion again in the 2019 Reining By The Bay Open Derby on a 229.5. His lifetime earnings stand at $82,200.

He is by Colonels Smoking Gun — “Gunner” — the NRHA Hall of Fame stallion and multi-million dollar sire whose own record includes NRHA Open Futurity Reserve Champion, USET Reining Finals Champion, NRBC Open Derby Champion, APHA World Champion Junior Reining Horse and APHA Superior Reining. Gunner was foaled in 1993, bred by Eric Story of Henagar, Alabama, by Colonelfourfreckle out of Katie Gun.

His dam, Princess In Diamonds, is a Million Dollar Dam by NRHA multi-million dollar sire Shining Spark out of Eyed Be A Princess, an earner of $77,000+ who placed 4th in the NRHA Open Futurity and 8th in the NRBC Open Derby. She is also the dam of Cromed Out Mercedes, the NRHA Million Dollar Sire standing alongside him at Silver Spurs, which makes the two stallions half-brothers on the same roster.

As a sire his record is young but moving. His first foal crop competed in 2022 and won close to $30,000, including a Level 4 Open Futurity finalist. Total offspring earnings now exceed $315,000. For a breeder, he offers direct Gunner blood on a Shining Spark mare, with APHA papers alongside AQHA — useful for anyone breeding for color as well as stop.
