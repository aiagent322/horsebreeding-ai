About Cromed Out Mercedes

Cromed Out Mercedes — 2005 Bay Stallion, AQHA, 14.3 hands. Known as “Curly.” Owned by and standing at Silver Spurs Equine LLC, Scottsdale AZ, and offered by ICSI breeding only. Full breeding information, video, and booking available at silverspursequine.com.

By Custom Crome (Crome Plated Jac/Hollywood Jac 86 x Another Greyhound/Greyhound Step — LTE $116,577; 1993 NRHA Futurity 3-Year-Old Open Champion; an NRHA Million Dollar Sire ranked by Equi-Stat among the top ten all-time leading reining sires, with offspring earnings in excess of $3,900,000 and producing sons and daughters whose own get have earned more than $2,600,000) out of Princess In Diamonds (by Shining Spark/Genuine Doc x Eyed Be A Princess/Eyed Be Impressive — $77,002; an Equi-Stat Leading Dam; 4th in the 2001 NRHA Futurity 3-Year-Old Open; 3rd in the 2001 WCEF Spectacular 3-Year-Old Open; 2002 NRBC 4 & 5-Year-Old Open finalist).

“Curly takes genetics to another level.” — Bob Loomis, NRHA Hall of Fame trainer, on Cromed Out Mercedes

His defining run came at the 2009 National Reining Breeders Classic in Katy, Texas. Ridden by Andrea Fappani, he tied to win the preliminary round on a 228 and then took the Open Classic Championship with a 232, finishing ahead of the previous year’s champions, Wimpys Little Chic and Shawn Flarida, who scored 231.5. It was Fappani’s second NRBC Open Championship aboard a Custom Crome son; he had been reserve on Custom Legend in 2005 and 2006 and champion on that horse in 2007. Speaking afterward, Fappani credited the support of owner Steve Simon and the quality of the Custom Crome line. At the time of the win the stallion was owned by Rancho El Fortin of Mexico.

That season also produced a Reserve Championship in the Reining By The Bay Open Derby and an NRHA Derby Open finals appearance, following a top-ten finish in the 2008 NRHA Open Futurity and a third-place tie at the 2008 Tulsa Reining Classic Open Futurity. His competition earnings closed at $146,650.

His record at stud has outgrown his record in the arena. Cromed Out Mercedes joined the NRHA Million Dollar Sire club in May 2019, at which point Equi-Stat credited him with 214 money-winning offspring. Silver Spurs now reports offspring earnings above $1,345,000. The get list runs deep rather than narrow: Mz Mercedes Whiz at $110,152 and Co-Reserve Champion of the NRHA Intermediate Open Futurity, Mercedes Kenz at $39,992, Cromed Out Sunset at $39,429, Diamonds N Mercedes at $39,279, Benz Smokin at $38,762, and Dunnies Luna Negra at $38,479, with winners in Italy, Germany and across the United States.

For breeders, the appeal is a concentrated reining pedigree at an accessible fee. Custom Crome sits on top, Shining Spark and Genuine Doc come through the dam, and the cross has already proven it produces futurity and derby horses at the national level. The trade-off is access: he is available by ICSI only, so mares must be managed through an ICSI program rather than conventional cooled or frozen breeding.
