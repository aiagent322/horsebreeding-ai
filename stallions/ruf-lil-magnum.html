About Ruf Lil Magnum

Ruf Lil Magnum — 2012 Sorrel Stallion, AQHA, 14.1 hands. Known as “The Rock.” Bred by Ann Salmon of Pilot Point, Texas. Owned by Silver Spurs Equine and standing at Scottsdale AZ. Full breeding information, video, and booking at silverspursequine.com.

He closed his show career with a World Championship, and he had to win it twice. In the 2018 Lucas Oil AQHA World Championship Show Senior Reining finals, a class of 44 entries with a purse over $41,000, he and Thiago Boechat of Purcell marked a 224.5. So did Wimpys Little Buddy and Martin Muehlstaetter. The two pairs agreed to a run-off and returned to the arena under the lights, and Ruf Lil Magnum took the title. He retired from competition as an AQHA World Champion.

Before that he was a consistent derby horse: 2017 NRHA Intermediate Open Derby Co-Champion, 2017 High Roller Reining Classic Intermediate Open Derby Champion, 2018 Oklahoma Ride & Slide Reserve Champion, 5th at the 2018 Reining By The Bay on a 228, and a 2015 NRHA Open Futurity finalist after winning the first go on a 224.5. Lifetime earnings $123,000+ and 33 AQHA points.

His sire is Magnum Chic Dream, one of the most established sires in reining and the same sire as Chic Dreamin on this roster. But the deeper story here is the bottom of the pedigree. His dam, Dunit A Lil Ruf, is an NRHA Million Dollar Dam with produce earnings around $1.25 million and an NRHA Limited Open Derby Championship of her own.

That makes Ruf Lil Magnum a half-brother to Patriot — earner of $233,368, 2020 AQHA World Champion Junior Reining Horse, NRHA Level 4 Open Derby Reserve Champion, NRBC Level 4 Open Derby Co-Champion, and winner of the 2022 Run For A Million Reining Championship with Nathan Piper. The same mare also produced Maximum Mag, the AQHA World Champion Amateur Reining Horse, and Ruf Lil Joe, NRBC Limited Open Derby Co-Reserve Champion and herself the dam of multiple world champion Inferno Joe. The granddam, A Bueno Poco Dunit, is an NRHA producer of more than $396,000.

His oldest foals were born in 2020 and his first crop began showing in 2023, with total offspring earnings now over $357,000. For a breeder the proposition is straightforward: a World Champion by a proven sire, out of one of the most productive reining broodmares of the last decade, at a fee well under what that page would normally command.
