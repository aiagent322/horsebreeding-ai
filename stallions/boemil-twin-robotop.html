About Boemil Twin Robotop

Boemil Twin Robotop — 2006 Appaloosa Stallion, ApHC, bay roan. Known as “Raiden.” Foaled in Italy on 25 March 2006 and bred by Emilio Bonoperti. Offered by frozen semen only through Silver Spurs Equine LLC, Scottsdale AZ. Full breeding information and booking at silverspursequine.com.

He is the NRHA’s all-time leading money-earning Appaloosa, and the record has a lineage of its own. Gunsmokes Skeeter held the title from 1986 with $30,174, shown exclusively by Terry Thompson. Some Kinda Shine passed that at the 2011 NRBC with Matt Mills, reaching $30,517. In 2014 Boemil Twin Robotop took it, and his lifetime earnings now stand at $37,489 — every dollar of it won at NRHA shows in Europe.

He was owned and shown in non-pro competition by Lara Lorengo of Italy, and trained from the age of two by Giuseppe Prevosti. In 2013 the pair took team gold at the FEI European Young Riders Reining Championship, Lorengo posting a 215.5 for an Italian team that also included Manuel Cortesi, Martina Roasio and Giovanni Masi de Vargas. She finished fourth individually, losing the bronze in a run-off.

The following year Prevosti rode him at the Alltech FEI World Equestrian Games in Normandy and qualified him for the individual finals — where, among 21 horse-and-rider combinations, he was the only Appaloosa. He finished 18th individually and 6th with Team Italy, and closed the season as European Appaloosa Championship Senior Reining Open Champion.

“He’s the best minded horse I’ve ever been around.” — Giuseppe Prevosti, 2014 FEI World Equestrian Games

His earlier European record is deep in the non-pro and limited divisions: European Appaloosa Reining Futurity Open Champion in both 2009 and 2010, Italian NRHA Derby champion at Limited, Intermediate and 25K Limited Non Pro levels in 2012, Italian Ladies Championship titles, and second in the 2014 Italian NRHA Derby Open.

For a breeder, he is a specific proposition rather than a general one. This is a colored stallion with a genuine open-level reining record against Quarter Horses, competing at the highest international level the sport offers. For an ApHC breeding program, or for anyone wanting color without giving up stop, he is close to unique. Breeding is by frozen semen only.
