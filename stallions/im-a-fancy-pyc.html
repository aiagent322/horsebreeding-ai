About Im A Fancy PYC

Im A Fancy PYC — 2012 Brown Stallion, AQHA, 15.2 hands, speed index 92. Entered stud in 2015. Owned by and standing at Silver Spurs Equine LLC, Purcell OK, by on-farm breeding only. Full breeding information and booking at silverspursoklahoma.com.

He is the roster’s one racing-bred stallion. By PYC Paint Your Wagon (Corona Cartel x Dashin Follies — SI 107, earner of $889,581; winner of the Texas Classic Futurity G1, Texas Classic Derby G1 and Heritage Place Derby G1; sire of more than 120 stakes winners and earners approaching $42 million) out of First Prize Fancy (SI 96, by Mr Jess Perry x First Prize Dash). The second dam, First Prize Dash, is a Broodmare of the Year and a Dash For Cash sister to First Down Dash, the all-time leading sire and a world champion earner of $857,256.

First Prize Fancy has produced 30 winners and seven or more black-type runners. Im A Fancy PYC’s full and half siblings out of her include Pyc Fun N Fancy (SI 104, $478,760, Heritage Place Derby G3), Stanley Cartel (SI 101, Vessels Maturity G1), First Fancy Prize (SI 98, $113,804), and Fast N Fancy (SI 92, $90,255). It is one of the deepest producing female families in American Quarter Horse racing.

On the track he won 5 races and $454,765, taking the Remington Park Oklahoma Bred Futurity (G3). At stud the record has been faster still. In 2019 he ranked as the third highest producing second-year sire, averaging over $18,600 per earner. In 2020 he was the #3 leading third-year sire with earners of $535,879. Silver Spurs now bills him as a 4.2 Million Dollar Sire, and he ranks sixth among the money-earning offspring of PYC Paint Your Wagon — a sire whose daughters have themselves produced Grade 1 winners including First Valiant Sign and champion Trump My Record.

For a breeder, the appeal is speed and a proven cross rather than a stopping or cow-bred pedigree. He is enrolled in the Black Gold and Oklahoma Bred incentive programs, which matters for anyone racing in Oklahoma. He is 5-panel N/N. Breeding is on-farm only at Purcell, with the 2026 collection schedule running Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays beginning February 2.
