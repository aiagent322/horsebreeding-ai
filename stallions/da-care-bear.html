About Da Care Bear

Da Care Bear — 2007 Bay Stallion, AQHA, 15 hands. Known as “Bear.” Standing at Silver Spurs Equine LLC, Scottsdale AZ, as a junior stallion. Full breeding information and booking at silverspursequine.com.

He is the odd one out on this roster, and that is the point of him. His sire, Boomernic, is an NRHA Hall of Fame stallion and Million Dollar Sire whose name is attached to reining futurity and derby champions. Bear went a different direction entirely: he spent his professional career as a rope horse, earning more than $6,200 and 91.5 AQHA performance points across heading and heeling, and taking the AQHA Superior Heading Horse title. The earnings figure understates the work, since it does not include weekend ropings.

Silver Spurs also notes that he is a capable reiner who simply never competed professionally in the discipline; Michelle Miola rode him in non-pro. That combination — a Hall of Fame reining pedigree expressed through a horse that made his living in the box — is the argument for him.

His dam, Ms Boon Boom Command, is by Docs Fritzie Command, himself by Fritz Command, and traces through Boon Bar and Anytime Lou. On the top side Boomernic brings Reminic and Docs Leavem Smoke. He is a full or half sibling in blood to two other Boomernic sons standing here, Boom Shernic and Haboomamatada.

His get are beginning to reach the market — the 2021 mare SS Grin N Bear It sold through the Double Down Horse & Bull Sale in 2025 — but he does not yet have a published offspring earnings record, and Silver Spurs does not list one. For a mare owner breeding for the rope pen who wants a proven performance pedigree behind it, he is available at the lowest fee on the roster.
