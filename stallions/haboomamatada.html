About Haboomamatada

Haboomamatada — 2004 Sorrel Stallion, AQHA, 14.2 hands. Known as “Matada.” Standing at Silver Spurs Equine LLC, Scottsdale AZ, as a junior stallion. Full breeding information and booking at silverspursequine.com.

His pedigree is a deliberate collision of two worlds. On top is Boomernic, NRHA Hall of Fame and a Million Dollar Sire of reining horses. On the bottom is Fives Little Lena, a daughter of Smart Little Lena — the NCHA Triple Crown winner and the most influential cutting sire of his generation. Silver Spurs describes Matada as the spitting image of his sire.

Fives Little Lena has produced across both sports. She is the NCHA producer of High Five Cat and the NRHA producer of Matada, and she has also produced to Playgun, including Winchester and Little Lena Playgun. That is a mare who reproduces ability regardless of which discipline the cross points at.

Matada showed in reining and did it well for two outings with Brett Stone: third in the 2007 Scottsdale Classic Open Futurity on a 214.5 in his very first showing, then a 218 at the High Roller Reining Classic the following year as a Derby finalist. His NRHA lifetime earnings are $1,560. Then Silver Spurs stopped showing him as a four-year-old, on the view that he was bred for cutting rather than reining and was being asked to do the wrong job.

“He stopped like a cow horse.” — Silver Spurs Equine, on his 2007 Scottsdale Classic run

The station’s own advice to breeders is specific: cross him on straight reining-bred mares to smooth out the stop, or breed him toward cow work and cutting where the Smart Little Lena half of the page does the talking. He is extremely athletic and good-minded, and the pitch is getting Boomernic blood without paying Boomernic prices.

His get so far have earned modestly and, tellingly, in three different events — $700 in barrel racing, $212 in roping and $49 in pole bending. The record is small but it points the same direction as the pedigree: versatility rather than specialization. Two other Boomernic sons stand alongside him here, Boom Shernic and Da Care Bear.
